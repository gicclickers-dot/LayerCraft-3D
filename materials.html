Materials We Work With

Explore common materials used in 3D printing.

🧵

PLA

A popular beginner-friendly printing material. Great for decorative objects, models, prototypes and everyday creative products.

🛠️

PETG

A versatile material with good durability and impact resistance. Suitable for functional parts and practical products.

⚙️

ABS

A tougher engineering plastic often used for durable components and functional prototypes. Requires suitable printing conditions.

🌈

TPU

A flexible material used for soft, bendable and rubber-like products such as flexible covers and protective parts.

💎

Resin

Liquid photopolymer resin can produce highly detailed models, miniatures and intricate decorative pieces.

🧪

Specialty Filaments

Special finishes and composite filaments can create unique textures, appearances and material properties.

Important: Material availability depends on the project, printer compatibility and requirements. Final material selection should be confirmed before production.
